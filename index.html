One old photo a day.
Everyone guesses.

Guesstory is a daily photo puzzle for families and close friends. Each day everyone in your group gets the same photo from the group's collection. Guess where it was taken and when, then see everyone's guesses and hear the story behind it.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

  1. Start a group and send one link to your family or friends. You choose who gets in.
  2. Add photos with the answer: where and when. Old prints, new photos, scan a snapshot from a shoebox, add the story.
  3. Every day, one photo becomes the same puzzle for everyone. Drop a pin on the map and pick a year.
  4. See the reveal together: the answer, everyone's guesses, the story, and a friendly score for the week.

Made for the whole family

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your photos live in your own iCloud and are only seen by the people you invite.

Easy to join

Tap a link and you're in. Large type, simple screens, and VoiceOver support so everyone can play.

Free to play

Free for groups of up to 4 people. A one-time Group Pass makes room for bigger groups. Joining and playing are always free.

Scores that stay friendly

Points for how close you were, no timers, and a fresh weekly scoreboard every Monday.

What you need

An iPhone 11 or newer (or a recent iPad) running iOS 26 or later, signed in to iCloud. Android phones can't join. Questions? See Support.
