Support

Guesstory is a daily photo puzzle for families and close friends: one photo a day, everyone guesses where and when, then see the reveal together.

Need a hand? Email SundaySpinSupport@gmail.com and we'll help you out.

Common questions

How do I join a group?

Tap the link someone sent you (in Messages or any chat app) on an iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later. If the app isn't installed, the link takes you to the App Store; install it and tap the link again. Enter your name, then wait for the host to let you in. If tapping the link does nothing or says it's unavailable, it may have been a single-use link that was already used. Ask for a new one.

What do I need to play?

An iPhone 11 or newer (or a recent iPad) running iOS 26 or later, signed in to iCloud. Android phones can't join. Settings → Help → Is everything set up? shows what needs attention.

How does a day work?

Each day one photo from the group's queue becomes the same puzzle for everyone. Drop a pin where you think it was taken and choose a year. The answer, everyone's guesses and the story appear once you have guessed. Results are final at midnight.

How are points worked out?

Up to 1,000 points: 500 for how close your pin was and 500 for the year. There is no timer. The person who added the photo says how precise the answer is (exact, town or general area), which sets how close counts as full points. If scores tie, the closer guess ranks higher.

Who can add photos?

Anyone in the group. Photos play in turn so everyone's photos come up. The host can reorder or remove photos (Group → Manage puzzles), and you can edit or withdraw your own photos before they play.

Why is my group limited to 4 people?

Free groups hold up to 4 people. Joining and playing are always free for the people invited. The group host (or anyone in the group) can buy the Group Pass once to make room for up to 50 people. One Group Pass covers every group you host or join.

I bought the Group Pass on another device.

Open Settings → Group Pass → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I remove someone or a photo?

The host can remove a member in Settings → Members, and remove a queued photo in Group → Manage puzzles. A removed person needs a brand-new invite to come back.

How do I report a concern?

Tap Report a concern on the reveal screen, in Manage puzzles or in Members, or email us. Nothing from your group is attached unless you write it.

How do I leave or delete a group?

Settings → Leave group, or (for the host) Delete group. Deleting removes the group from iCloud.

Will I lose my groups if I get a new phone or reinstall?

No. Your groups are stored in iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account and Guesstory restores them. The first launch shows a short “Restoring” screen while they download.

I'm not getting notifications.

Turn them on in the app under Settings, and allow them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Guesstory. Alerts about other people's activity are best-effort and may arrive late; the daily reminder is scheduled on your phone.

Are my photos private?

Yes. They live in your group's iCloud and are visible only to the people in the group. We can't see them. See the Privacy Policy.

Still stuck?

Email SundaySpinSupport@gmail.com. It helps to tell us your iPhone model and iOS version.
