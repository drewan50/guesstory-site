Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Guesstory does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We run no servers, so we cannot see your photos, answers, stories, names, guesses or comments.

What the app does

Guesstory is a daily photo puzzle for families and close friends. People in a private group add photos with the answer (where and when), and each day everyone guesses the same photo.

Where your information is stored

Everything you and your group create (photos, answers, stories, names, guesses, scores, reactions, comments and reports) is stored in Apple's iCloud, in the group host's iCloud storage, and on the devices of the people in the group. It is shared only through Apple's CloudKit service. We, the developer, run no servers and have no way to read it.

Who can see what

  • Only people invited to the group can see its photos, answers, guesses and comments. Nothing is public or searchable.
  • The host approves who joins, and can remove photos, comments and members. Anyone can leave a group at any time.
  • Members can write a short comment (up to 140 characters) on a photo once they have guessed. A comment is shown with the name of the person who wrote it, to the people in that group only. Its author can edit or delete it.
  • Anyone in a group can report a photo or a person. The report goes to the group's host, inside the group; the app shows it only to the host and the person who made it. It is stored in the group's iCloud like everything else in the group, and we never receive it.
  • If you choose to hide someone's photos and comments for yourself, that choice is kept on your own device only.
  • The host can delete the group, which removes it from iCloud. Copies disappear from members' devices when they next sync.
  • Names are only what each person types when joining. Guesstory does not read your Apple Account name, email or phone number.

Photos and location

Before a photo is uploaded, the app re-encodes it on your device and removes its location and other embedded information. If you pick a photo from your library, the app may read its location and date on your device to suggest an answer; you confirm or change it, and that information is never uploaded or sent anywhere. The answer you enter is stored as separate fields visible only to the group.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you choose to scan a printed photo.
  • Photos: only the photos you choose in the system picker. The app does not browse your library.
  • Notifications: optional daily reminders and group activity alerts. A reminder may include the name of your group. They never include anyone's answer, score or comment.
  • Guesstory does not ask for access to your location, contacts or microphone.

Maps

Map tiles and place search are provided by Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive these searches.

Purchases

The optional Group Pass is bought through the App Store and handled entirely by Apple. We do not see your payment details. Other members of your groups can see that a group is unlocked by a member's Group Pass.

Children

A family may include children with their own Apple Accounts. Guesstory collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Advertising and tracking

None. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code.

Your choices

You control your data: remove a photo, leave a group, or delete a group you host. Deleting the app removes its local copies. Data stored in iCloud can also be managed in iOS Settings under your name → iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
